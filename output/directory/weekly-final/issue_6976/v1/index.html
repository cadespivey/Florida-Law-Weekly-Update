Kind: court_rule

Status: operative

The Supreme Court of Florida adopted comprehensive amendments to the Florida Rules of Civil Procedure, effective April 1, 2026. Key changes include: expanded scope of discovery under Rule 1.280(b) permitting deposition of non-parties with demonstrated materiality; revised summary judgment standard under Rule 1.510(c) requiring evidentiary burden-shifting analysis; mandatory early case management conferences under Rule 1.200(f) within 90 days of filing; revised service-of-process methods under Rule 1.070(d) allowing electronic service with recipient consent; and updated venue-transfer rules under Rule 3.030 shifting default venue determination to 60 days post-answer. These amendments affect all civil actions filed on or after April 1, 2026. Actions pending prior to that date continue under prior rules unless the court exercises discretion to apply amended procedures.
