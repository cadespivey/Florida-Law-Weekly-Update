# October 2026 evidence-baseline delivery

This release implements the scope in [EVIDENCE_BASELINE.md](EVIDENCE_BASELINE.md).
It publishes five reviewed reports, rather than contemporaneous captures of the
historical websites. Originals, extracted text, hashes, retrieval timestamps,
source-stated dates, review receipts and documented conflicts remain in the shared
local research-history database. Only allowlisted report exports are public.

The directory has 181 unique judges and clerks, with judicial-assistant fields,
contacts, assignments and official procedure sources. It covers the Supreme Court,
six DCAs, and the seven requested counties within circuits 4, 7 and 8. Its actual
October 11–12 observation is disclosed on historical reports; it is not backdated.

The entire Sunshine Florida Statutes compilation is retained: 49 titles,
638 chapters and 24,993 indexed sections. Compilation identity is preserved;
selected 2026 session laws and operative-date findings are separate. Collection
of all chapters does not mean every provision received substantive legal review.
Commercial civil and shared procedures received priority. Existing criminal and
diversion material is preserved without requiring exhaustive additional analysis.

Known limitations remain visible in each report: selected historical discovery,
Baker/Union operational-page HTTP 403 responses, thirteen unavailable Seventh
Circuit attachments, the inaccessible Clay auction site, and bounded agency-rule
coverage. Conflicts are evidence records, not determinations of controlling law.
The original acquisition remains 215/217 evidence-valid; supported report
finalization does not rewrite or falsely close its two blocked operational checks.

Finalized captures 4267–4271 establish the five reports in chronological order;
October 11 is the latest finalized-report comparison baseline. The export contains
immutable capture and report checksums. Partial future research cannot advance
that baseline. Weekly periods remain Monday–Sunday.

Validation: all 123 rendered summaries and their source links matched the reviewed
export payloads; all 17 court filters and 30/60/90/180-day shortcuts were exercised.
Browser checks covered reload/back/forward, invalid and empty ranges, and 320px /
1280px layouts. Publication tests retain checksum, citation, date and finalization
gates. Deployment and live verification are recorded separately after release.
