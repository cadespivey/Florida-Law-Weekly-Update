# Evidence baseline policy — user clarification, October 12, 2026

This policy governs the active five-snapshot baseline and supersedes conflicting
research-depth or conflict-resolution requirements in older plans/instructions.
The deliverable is an evidence snapshot for comparison, not resolution of disputed
authorities. Keep the active goal; do not restart research or discard prior work.

## Scope and priority

Retain the entire Florida Statutes compilation and the full requested judicial,
assistant and clerk roster: Supreme Court, six DCAs, and Baker, Clay, Duval,
Nassau, Putnam, St. Johns and Union counties. Retain the five requested reports,
July 1 opening baseline and changes through August 1, September 1, October 1 and
October 11. Preserve pinned cutoffs, inclusive periods, actual acquisition times,
Sunday weekly cutoffs and the last finalized-report comparison baseline.

Prioritize substantive review of civil procedure, jurisdiction, contracts,
business entities, commercial transactions, property, foreclosure, collections,
remedies, evidence, appellate practice and relevant court administration.

For primarily criminal/diversionary judges, verify contacts, assignments and their
official individual procedure links. Do not exhaustively review criminal plea
forms, sentencing, drug-court or veterans-court manuals. Review civil duties and
shared procedures (communications, scheduling, orders, remote appearances) to the
extent applicable. Preserve previously collected/reviewed material. Do not follow
low-priority attachments merely to complete an exhaustive document tree.

## Evidence states and disagreements

Track these dimensions independently in receipts and report coverage:

- `collection_status`: collected, linked_only, unavailable. Collected means raw
  bytes and extracted text, URL, retrieval timestamp and checksums are preserved.
- `content_review`: reviewed, partially_reviewed, not_reviewed. Describe the exact
  pages/sections and applicable civil/shared scope; an import is not a review.
- `conflicts`: each entry lists both source statements with their citations,
  original artifacts, retrieval times, and stated revision/effective dates (or
  explicitly unknown dates), plus a short neutral discrepancy description.

A verified assertion means the official source states it, not that it legally
controls. Do not select a controlling source or infer supersession without an
express source statement. A documented disagreement does not make an otherwise
completed source review incomplete and does not block publication. Preserve
multiple contact values with source labels rather than merging them into one
unqualified current contact. Missing evidence, incorrect attribution, broken
provenance and inaccurate records still require repair or an honest gap record.

Existing citation validators remain mandatory. Their verified procedure status
means the required scoped review and individual link association are verified;
use `content_review` and applicability to disclose unreviewed criminal material.
Never claim an entire low-priority document was reviewed when only its link or
shared sections were checked. No weakening checksum, quote or identity checks.

## Delivery criteria

1. Compilation coverage reconciled for all Florida Statutes, with version/source
   identity preserved; separate compilation text from supported operative-date
   findings. Do not promise exhaustive statutory interpretation.
2. Full requested roster reconciled with official current assignments and contact
   sources; individual procedure links and civil/shared review documented.
3. Commercial civil and shared procedural baseline reviewed; source conflicts
   recorded without adjudication. Low-priority exhaustive reviews are unnecessary.
4. Each requested report contains supported findings, source links and explicit
   historical limitations. Distinguish effective events from publication and first
   observation. Never backdate current rosters, pretend unavailable history exists,
   or infer no changes from missing evidence. Document unavailable reconstruction
   as a limitation, not a requirement to resolve unknowable history before delivery.
5. Save originals, findings and state distinctions in the shared history database.
   Review source fidelity and rendering, finalize the five supported reports, then
   publish and verify live content, date filters, citations and directory links.

Final review checks accurate evidence representation and scope completion, not
which conflicting authority controls. Report known limitations conspicuously.
